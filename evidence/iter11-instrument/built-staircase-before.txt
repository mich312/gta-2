# SCORE 135.0, in weighted tiles of defect, against TOTAL 24 candidates.
# TOTAL is the count. It compared straight back through the loop until iteration 9, which
# taught country-outside-blocks to ask the bake's own wildness field before claiming ground
# "was never asked what it is" — so a number from iterations 5-8 is on the OLD instrument and
# does not compare with one from this. Both series, over every bake the loop has, are in
# evidence/iter9-instrument/history.txt; the deltas BETWEEN iterations are unchanged.
# SCORE is what moves when a defect gets SMALLER without going away — which a count cannot see.
# SCORE is an area, so the signature covering the most ground dominates it; a small signature's
# progress shows in its own `tiles` column, which is the same measurement unweighted.
# DRAWN 3.8 is the part of SCORE a renderer actually PUTS ON THE SCREEN.
# SCORE counts defects in the TILE PLANE — which is also what collision drives against — and
# a defect can be in the tile plane and painted over: a quay stepping every three tiles is
# repainted on a chord by the coast, bank or deck curve and cannot be seen. Those tiles stay
# in SCORE, because a renderer change can expose them again without one tile of ground moving,
# and are subtracted in DRAWN, because a reviewer sent to look at them will see nothing.
# The two differ only where a signature has MEASURED its own drawing, which today is
# `built-staircase` alone; every other signature is a defect in the ground, and ground is drawn.
